Price Checker

Instant price & stock lookup for Point of Sale and Inventory — scan a barcode, get the customer-facing price and current stock in under a second.

Key Features

🛒 Native POS Integration

Adds a dedicated Price Checker button right in the Point of Sale control panel. Cashiers can look up any product's price and stock without leaving the active order screen.

🔖 Barcode Scanner Support

Works with hardware barcode scanners in both the backend (Inventory) and inside POS, using Odoo's native BarcodeReader service — plus manual barcode/name entry when a scanner isn't handy.

💰 Tax-Inclusive Pricing

Shows the real price a customer will pay, computed from sales taxes only — purchase taxes are excluded so the number on screen always matches the receipt.

🏢 Company-Scoped Stock

On-hand quantity reflects only the current company's warehouses — built for multi-company and multi-branch deployments where stock must never leak across companies.

Also Included

  • Real-time stock status badges — In Stock / Low Stock / Out of Stock
  • Product image preview alongside price and stock results
  • Audible feedback (success / not-found beep) on every scan
  • Free-text search by product name, internal reference, or barcode
  • Read-only backend list & form views under the Inventory menu for quick browsing
  • Bilingual-ready interface (Arabic / English)
